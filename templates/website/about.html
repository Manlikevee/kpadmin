About KP Global

A church family that loves God, loves people and makes room for everyone.

We exist to help people know God and make Him known. That means worship that is honest, teaching that is rooted in the Bible, and a community where nobody walks alone.

KP Global began as a small prayer gathering and has grown into a church family across several campuses and online. Our name and our flame remind us of the same thing: a faith that is alive, warm and meant to be shared.

On any Sunday you will find families, students, singles and seniors worshipping side by side. We are not perfect people. We are people who have found grace and want to pass it on.

What we believe

The convictions that shape everything we do.

The Bible

We believe the Bible is God’s inspired Word, and the final authority for what we believe and how we live.

God

We believe in one God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus is the Son of God, who died for our sins, rose again, and will return.

Salvation

We believe salvation is God’s gift, received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit lives in every believer, giving power to live for Christ and serve others.

The Church

We believe the church is the body of Christ, called to worship, grow together and share the gospel.
