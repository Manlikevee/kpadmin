I’m new here

We would love to meet you. Here is what a Sunday at KP Global looks like.

What to expect

1

Arrive

Our ushers will greet you at the door and help you find a seat or the children’s check-in.

2

Worship

About 30 minutes of songs and prayer. Join in however you feel comfortable.

3

The Word

A practical message from the Bible, usually around 40 minutes.

4

Connect

Stay for refreshments. Someone from our welcome team would love to meet you.

Before you come

What should I wear?

Whatever you are comfortable in. You will see everything from jeans to suits.

What about my kids?

Children’s church runs during the main service for ages 3 to 12, with trained, checked volunteers.

How long is the service?

About two hours, including worship and the message.

Do I have to give?

Not at all. Giving is for members and regulars who want to. As a guest, just enjoy the service.
